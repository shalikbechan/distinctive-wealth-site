05 — Estate & Legacy Planning

Structure your legacy with intention.

Estate and legacy planning helps ensure that your wealth is structured to provide for the people who matter most, in a way that reflects your wishes.

Planning beyond your lifetime

Without proper planning, an estate can face delays, unnecessary costs, and financial strain on the people left behind. We help clients consider how their estate is structured — including liquidity to cover estate costs, and how wealth is intended to transfer to beneficiaries — so that their legacy is protected as intended.

Estate planning works closely with life insurance and investment planning, forming part of a coordinated long-term financial strategy.

What We Help You Consider

  • Estate planning
  • Legacy planning
  • Estate liquidity
  • Wealth transfer planning
  • Family protection

In Detail

1 Estate Liquidity

  • Planning for estate duty, taxes and administration costs
  • Helps avoid the forced sale of assets to cover costs
  • Structured cover to provide liquidity when required

2 Wealth Transfer Planning

  • Considers how assets pass to beneficiaries
  • Coordinated with wills and other legal structures
  • Aims to preserve value through the transfer process

3 Legacy Planning

  • Reflects personal wishes for how wealth is used
  • Consideration of family and philanthropic intentions
  • Reviewed as family circumstances evolve

4 Family Protection

  • Financial security for dependants after your lifetime
  • Coordinated with life insurance and trust structures
  • Consideration of guardianship and provision for minors
