06 — Business Insurance & Assurance

Protect the business you have built.

Business insurance solutions are designed to protect key people, business continuity and the long-term value of your business, for the benefit of owners, employees and stakeholders.

Protecting business continuity

A business can be significantly affected by the loss, disability or severe illness of a key person or owner. We help business owners understand the solutions available to manage this risk — from key-person cover to buy-and-sell agreements that support an orderly transfer of ownership.

These solutions are structured around the specific ownership, operational and financial structure of each business.

What We Help You Consider

  • Key-person protection
  • Business life cover
  • Buy-and-sell protection
  • Business continuity
  • Business disability and severe illness protection
  • Protection for business owners and employees

In Detail

1 Key-Person Protection

  • Cover on individuals critical to business operations or revenue
  • Helps offset the financial impact of losing a key person
  • Supports business stability during a transition period

2 Buy-and-Sell Protection

  • Funds the transfer of a deceased or incapacitated owner's share
  • Structured according to shareholder or partnership agreements
  • Supports an orderly change in ownership

3 Business Continuity

  • Financial support to maintain operations after a disruptive event
  • Considers contingent liabilities and cash-flow needs
  • Coordinated with broader business risk planning

4 Disability & Severe Illness Protection

  • Cover for business owners and key employees
  • Supports the business through a qualifying event
  • Can be structured alongside personal protection cover
