01 — Life & Long-Term Insurance

Protection for what matters most.

Our life and long-term insurance solutions are designed to help protect families, financial commitments and long-term plans — providing financial security for the people who depend on you.

Why life insurance matters

Life insurance provides a financial safety net for the people and responsibilities that depend on you. Whether it is supporting a family, covering financial commitments such as a bond, or protecting a business, the right cover can help ensure that your plans continue even if you are no longer there to provide for them.

We help clients understand the different types of life and long-term insurance available, and how these solutions can be structured to align with personal circumstances, family responsibilities and long-term financial plans.

What We Help You Consider

  • Life cover
  • Death benefits
  • Family and dependant protection
  • Estate and legacy protection
  • Long-term insurance planning

In Detail

1 Life Cover & Death Benefits

  • Lump-sum cover payable to beneficiaries or dependants
  • Structured to help settle outstanding debts and financial commitments
  • Can be aligned with income replacement needs

2 Family & Dependant Protection

  • Financial support for spouses, children and dependants
  • Cover structured around household and lifestyle needs
  • Consideration of changing family circumstances over time

3 Estate & Legacy Protection

  • Support for estate costs, including estate duty and liquidity needs
  • Helps preserve the value of an estate for beneficiaries
  • Aligns with broader estate and legacy planning

4 Long-Term Insurance Planning

  • Regular reviews as personal or family circumstances change
  • Coordination with other protection and investment solutions
  • Access to solutions through established insurance providers
