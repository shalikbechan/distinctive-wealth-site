Discovery

Life, health-linked protection, education and business solutions available through our partnership with Discovery.

About This Partnership

Broadening the protection and investment solutions available to our clients.

Distinctive Wealth Management works with Discovery to give clients access to a wide range of life, disability, severe illness, education, business and retirement solutions. Where appropriate to a client's circumstances and objectives, we can assist in understanding the relevant Discovery solutions and how they may fit within a broader financial plan.

Not every solution is automatically suited to every client — our role is to help you understand what is available and whether it is appropriate for your situation.

1 Life Insurance

  • Life cover
  • Death benefits
  • Family and dependant protection
  • Estate and legacy protection

2 Disability & Income Protection

  • Capital disability cover
  • Income protection
  • Temporary and permanent disability protection
  • Protection against loss of earning ability

3 Severe Illness Cover

  • Cover for critical/severe illnesses
  • Lump-sum protection following qualifying illnesses
  • Financial support during recovery and lifestyle changes

4 Education Protection

  • Protection of children's education costs
  • Education funding following death, disability or other qualifying life events

5 Business Insurance

  • Business life cover
  • Key-person protection
  • Business disability and severe illness protection
  • Protection for business owners and continuity

6 Retirement & Investments

  • Retirement annuities
  • Flexible investments
  • Endowments
  • Guaranteed-return investments
  • Offshore/global investments
